# HR management rollout

- [ ] Phase 1: employee records linked to existing accounts, attendance including field duty, leave requests and balances, secure approvals, audit trail, tests and user documentation.
- [ ] Phase 2: linked consignment/trip assignments, recruitment, onboarding/exit workflows, private HR documents and configurable approvals.
- [ ] Phase 3: role-specific appraisals, training, certificates, reminders and authorized reports.
- [ ] Phase 4: payroll review and approval with verified effective-dated Ghana statutory rules and duplicate-safe Accounts integration.

Later phases follow verified Phase 1 workflows; statutory payroll requires verified rules before activation.
